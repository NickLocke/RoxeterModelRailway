# Points

Points can be commanded to move in two ways - either by a control switch on the panel, or by the route setting process.

Prototypically, all form of panel have control switches, although in Roxeter they have been omitted from the OCS Panel at Roxeter Low Level. 

IFS panels such as Carvil Junction have two position switches for the points, meaning that they are always commanded to either the Normal or Reverse position. NX and OCS panels have three position switches. The left and right positions lock the points Normal or Reverse respectively, whilst the centre (usual) position allows the points to be moved by the route setting process. If the points are locked in either position by the switch, then a route needing that position can be set through the points, but the route setting cannot move teh points to the other position.

Points which are free to move (not locked by the control switch) can be moved by the route setting process. Once moved they will be locked in position and incapable of being moved by the control switch.

Points cannot be moved if the track circuit monitoring them is occupied.

Points cannot be moved if any signal protecting them is displaying an off aspect, or is waiting for approach locking to release.

Points feed their Normal or Reverse position back through detection.
