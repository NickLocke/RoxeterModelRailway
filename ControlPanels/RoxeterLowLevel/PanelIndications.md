# Panel Indications

Numbers listed here relate to the LED number in the sequence of 255 LEDs that can be connected to the CANARGB.

## Points

The only direct indication of points on the panel is the out of correspondence indication when the position is not known and/or the points are moving. The indication is given by the two route lights nearest to the points flashing alternately.
